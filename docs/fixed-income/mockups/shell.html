One shell, two desks — Options & Fixed Income tabs

A top-level asset-class tab layer sits above the workspace rail. Switching the tab swaps the left-rail workspace set and the workspaces it renders; the surrounding chrome and the pricing engine are shared. The two desks never bleed into each other, but a trader moves between them in one click.
