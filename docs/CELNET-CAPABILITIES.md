# Celnet — Capabilities, Architecture & CelNet Integration

> Celnet is a state-of-the-art FX-**options** pricing & risk platform: ultra-low-latency, mission-critical and hot-upgradable. One clean, API-first contract sits behind a React/WebGPU trader GUI, an Excel add-in, a Rust SDK and a CLI.

> **The competitive thesis.** Celnet is not a thin challenger closing gaps — it is a functionally complete, evidence-backed superset of what a derivatives desk stitches together today: vanilla → the full first-generation exotics → structured & path-dependent products → American/Bermudan early exercise → correlated multi-asset baskets → an LSV booking model + standalone Heston, all on **one unversioned contract**, parity-gated against independent oracles, reachable **bit-identically** from five clients. It pairs that catalogue depth with edges the deep-catalogue incumbents structurally lack — an open in-engine quant SDK, a pinned zero-alloc nanosecond hot core, server-side hierarchical risk, and an honest evidence trail (a runnable parity matrix, frozen golden tables, mutation + fuzz). **All 18-products are reachable across the client surfaces, proven by [`CLIENT-PARITY-MATRIX.md`](clients/CLIENT-PARITY-MATRIX.md).**

## How Celnet fits together

- **One contract, every surface.** Every capability lives in a single unversioned API — five gRPC services with a byte-identical WebSocket JSON mirror — consumed by the GUI, Excel, the SDK and the CLI, so a value is bit-identical across surfaces and all 18-products reach all five clients. → *[API & Client Parity](celnet-capabilities/09-api-contract-parity.md)*
- **A complete catalogue.** Vanilla, five smile families (VV/SABR/SVI/SSVI/eSSVI), the full 18-product exotic Instrument through American/Bermudan and correlated baskets, and an LSV booking model — each parity-gated against an independent oracle. → *[Quant Coverage](celnet-capabilities/04-quant-coverage.md)*
- **Adaptable by design.** Pluggable market-data and product adapters flow in; bit-identical pricing surfaces flow out; the same codebase deploys three ways across **34 one-way-acyclic crates**. → *[System Architecture](celnet-capabilities/03-system-architecture.md)*, *[CelNet Integration](celnet-capabilities/12-celnet-integration.md)*
- **Extensible at the core.** Desks run their own private-IP models inside the engine through the Open Quant SDK — the native (Tier-0) and WebAssembly (Tier-2 wasmi) tiers ship and are gated. → *[Extensibility](celnet-capabilities/05-extensibility-plugins.md)*
- **Whole-firm risk.** Book-shaped scenario risk, a firm-wide position-fact cube rolling up vanilla and exotic legs, FRTB-SA capital and internal XVA are zooms of one model. → *[Risk Management](celnet-capabilities/06-risk-management.md)*
- **Scales out, proven on localhost.** A Raft-replicated log (election, truncation, compaction, InstallSnapshot) gives bit-identical replay; a lock-free SPMC ring fans each pair's tick to every subscriber; cross-fleet risk equals the single-node aggregate to 1e-12. → *[Scalability & Scale-Out](celnet-capabilities/08-scalability-scaleout.md)*

## Honest boundary

Celnet's capability claims are grounded in shipped code, and its evidence is labelled. The following capabilities are **deploy/live-gated by design and are never claimed as proven in-repo** — they are seamed and ready, proven at deploy, not asserted here:

- **Cross-host wire p99 / kernel-bypass NIC latency** — deploy-gated; in-repo proves the in-core §1.2 truth-gate + loopback benches only.
- **CUDA/NVIDIA ABSOLUTE GPU throughput + ≤50ms exotic + Workload-A/B absolute numbers** — deploy-gated. M4 Metal lacks f64 ⇒ in-repo proves **correctness + RATIOS only** (M4/Lavapipe). Never claim f64 on Metal.
- **The §11 ABSOLUTE wire-latency SLOs** — deploy-gated (in-repo proves the §1.2 truth-gate + loopback only).
- **The entire live JVM CelNet estate lifecycle** (sidecar / FX_OPTION / inferred hops / tenant overlays) — deploy/live-gated; in-repo has the seams + adapters only.
- **Raft §6 dynamic membership / cross-DC transport / real network partitions** — deploy-gated; in-repo proves correctness/quorum/framing on localhost multi-process only.
- **Plugin Tier-1 signed-.so (stabby) + Tier-3 Landlock/seccomp** — designed-only; only Tier-0 native + Tier-2 wasmi are shipped.
- **XVA (CVA/DVA/FVA, celnet-xva)** — internal-only, NO client/wire surface, **synthetic netting sets only**; live CSAs/collateral/wrong-way risk are deploy-gated.
- **Multi-source surface aggregation** — the blend/staleness/divergence ALGORITHM is built and gated; live multi-vendor quote VALUES are an integration/deploy target, not in-repo data.
- **MC-priced products** (TARF, accumulator, discrete lookback, basket/best-of/worst-of, American via LSM) carry a **price std-error** — never labelled "machine-precision"; that bar is reserved for analytic/PDE/golden-gated products.
