5. How CelNet Replicates: Determinism & Chaos Immunity

DIFFERENTIATOR: FLAP RESILIENT

Exploratory Pre-Vote Consensus Phase

The Trading Problem: In standard distributed consensus, when a temporary network glitch isolates a follower node, its election timer expires, it increments its term, and broadcasts vote requests. When the partition heals, its inflated term forces the healthy cluster leader to step down, causing trading freezes and re-election spikes.

CelNet's Solution: Before incrementing its term, the candidate runs an exploratory, non-binding Pre-Vote straw poll. Because healthy majority nodes are still receiving active heartbeats from the true leader, they reject the Pre-Vote. The flapping node never increments its term, and the active trading leader continues processing orders without disruption.

DIFFERENTIATOR: ZERO FALSE PROGRESS

Durable Commit Watermarking & Log Reconcile

The Audit Invariant: In our adversarial chaos verification, when a 5-node cluster is severed into a minority partition (2 nodes) and a majority partition (3 nodes), the minority leader attempts to propose order updates.

Upon partition healing, the higher-term leader reconciles logs and automatically truncates divergent uncommitted entries. All nodes converge to exact mathematical state parity.
