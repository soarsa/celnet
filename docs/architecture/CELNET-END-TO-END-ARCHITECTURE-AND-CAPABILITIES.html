Operating entirely in CPU registers and L1/L2 cache under #![forbid(unsafe_code)], the core engine prices vanilla options in 22.5 ns and generates complete 13-Greek analytic risk vectors in 36.5 ns with zero heap allocations, eliminating GC pauses, memory leaks, and thread contention on the pricing loop.

2. End-to-End Trading Lifecycle & Wire Topology ZERO-ALLOCATION INGESTION TO EXECUTION

Every tick, RFQ, and execution flows through a deterministic, allocation-free pipeline. Pinned CPU cores process events without context switching or heap locks, ensuring sub-microsecond tick-to-trade responsiveness.

Phase 1: Ingest & Ring Buffer

Venue feeds (EBS, CME iLink3, Bloomberg B-PIPE) arrive via UDP multicast. Kernel-bypass drivers copy raw network frames directly into user-space SPMC ring buffers in 11.3 ns. Messages are 128B aligned to prevent cache-line bouncing across NUMA nodes.

Phase 2: In-Core ALU & Greeks

The numerical engine consumes the order without memory copies. Pinned ALU registers calculate forward BSM carry, evaluate SABR/SVI volatility smiles, and generate the full 13-Greek risk vector in 36.5 ns using hardware fsqrt instructions.

Phase 3: Risk Cube & Distribution

Trade ticks hit the in-memory multidimensional risk cube, updating desk and legal entity limits in < 800 ns. The fill commits to the Raft consensus journal while SBE flyweights fan out updates over /dev/shm to WebGPU GUI and Excel in 14.46 ns.

SBE Zero-Copy Flyweight Memory Layout (ADR-0008)

Simple Binary Encoding (SBE) defines flat, schema-versioned memory structures where message fields reside at fixed byte offsets. The consumer casts raw memory pointers directly into Rust structs (#[repr(C, packed)]), achieving true zero-copy decoding in 0.00 ns with no memory allocation.

5. Quantitative Coverage & Model Formulation MATHEMATICAL PURITY · ADR-0012 UNIFIED SEAM

CelNet resolves the historic fragmentation of quantitative libraries by expressing FX options, interest rate curves, equity derivatives, and commodities through a single generalized forward carry seam (ADR-0012).

FX Options & Parametric Smile Families

Generalized BSM Kernel: Evaluated in forward space: df · [F Φ(d1) - K Φ(d2)]. By probability symmetry Φ(-x) = 1 - Φ(x), error function calls are cut from 4 to 2, halving transcendental instruction cycles.

13 Analytic Greeks (36.5 ns Single Pass): First-order (ΔSpot, ΔFwd, Vega, Theta, ρdom, ρfor); Second-order (Gamma, Vanna, Volga, Charm); Third-order (Speed, Zomma, Color).

Fixed Income & Multi-Curve Term Structures

Horner Discount Recurrence: Cashflows c_k · (1 + y/f)^{-(w + k - 1)} are factored into D_0 = (1 + y/f)^{-w} and D_k = D_{k-1} · v, replacing 60 transcendental powf calls with 1 powf and 59 multiplications.

Post-LIBOR Framework: OIS risk-free discounting (SOFR, €STR, SONIA) decoupled from tenor basis forwarding curves with cross-currency basis adjustment and 10-bucket Key Rate DV01 ladders.
