The Institutional Challenge: Unbounded static skews ($s_{\max} > h$) silently create through-mid quotes. When quoting fast-moving crosses across multiple ECNs, an unconstrained inventory skew will shift an offer below the prevailing mid-market price, allowing toxic flow to sweep stale inventory at a discount.

CelNet Production Solution: The implementation of SK-P1 dynamically enforces $|s| \le \min(s_{\max}, \lambda \cdot h)$. Outbound quotes are mathematically prohibited from crossing mid-market. Furthermore, the CA-G6 lock-free per-instrument quote lock provides sub-microsecond single-instrument halt capability, safeguarding the desk against runaway quoting without interrupting unrelated currency pairs or bond curves.

CRITIQUE PASSED

The Institutional Challenge: The "double-count hazard": an autonomous auto-hedger transfers risk externally while the quoting engine skews outbound prices to attract natural counter-interest. If inventory skew is driven by raw nominal quantity rather than limit utilization, the desk experiences aggressive double-hedging, whipsawing positions and generating excess slippage.

CelNet Production Solution: Under SK-P2, inventory skew is normalized against the firm's approved risk limit capacity ($u = q / ext{limit\_cap} \in [-1, 1]$). Skew intensity scales non-linearly with limit utilization, automatically decaying as auto-hedging actions bleed inventory back below critical risk bands.

// Limit Utilization Skew Damping let util = (ctx.inventory / limit_cap).clamp(-1.0, 1.0); let intensity = util.abs().powf(utilization_power); let skew = -util.signum() * max_skew * intensity;
