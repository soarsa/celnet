CelNet architecture — current state & the one-contract target

CelNet prices FX options, cross-asset options and linear products through a single, unversioned pricing + risk contract (celnet-core::contract, ADR-0007 / ADR-0017 Phase A1). Fixed Income is built and live but still runs on a parallel, siloed path. This page maps the current topology, the fully-unified target, and the phased plan that collapses the FI silos onto the one contract.

§1 Current state — one unified options path, one Fixed-Income silo

FX / equity / commodity / crypto vanilla and linear products already converge on the shared carry seam and the one contract. Fixed Income flows through an entirely parallel path: its own standalone pricing function, its own proto message, its own RPC, its own risk service — and no streaming. The Fixed-Income cluster is drawn in coral.

§2 Target state — Fixed Income on the one contract

Every asset class — including Fixed Income — prices and risks through the single celnet-core::contract seam: one Priceable, one Instrument.product wire, one price RPC, one aggregate_risk roll-up, one streaming subscription. The edges the FI unification adds are drawn in coral.

the one contract, its spine & clients newly-unified — Fixed Income joins the contract shared foundation

The target is not a new architecture — it is the completion of the one already landed. celnet-core::contract defines a single asset-class-agnostic seam: Priceable::price / Priceable::risk over a ResolvedMarket whose discounting is the shared DiscountCurve trait — a flat two-rate carry for FX, a bootstrapped celnet-rates curve for FI, interchangeable behind the trait. The RiskMeasure enum already carries both a OptionGreeks tag and a reserved RateLadder tag; Phase A1 proved the re-seat is byte-identical on FX vanilla (f6022dd).

Two lanes complete the unification. be-rates-wire-arms gives the FI leaves (OisEngine, SwapEngine, BondEngine) a ProductEngine + Priceable impl, adds FI products as additive arms of Instrument.product (one price RPC — no version, per ADR-0007), and retires the standalone price_rates(). fe-fi-migration then collapses the FI silos: aggregate_rates_risk folds into the one aggregate_risk emitting RiskMeasure::RateLadder into celnet-risk-cube, the streaming session gains an AssetClass::Rates arm, and the separate RatesInstrument / PriceRates messages are removed. When both land, Fixed Income is indistinguishable from FX at the contract boundary — one wire, one dispatch, one risk cube, one stream.

§3 Delivery plan — the phased board lanes (current → target)

The unification ships as disjoint, coordinator-gated board lanes. The one contract (central-core Phase-A1) is the landed foundation; the FI lanes and the platform/console lanes build on it.
