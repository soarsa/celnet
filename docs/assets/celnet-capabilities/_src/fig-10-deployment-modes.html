Egress governor — between the pricer and the estate distributor
Continuous prices pass through a conflating governor before fan-out: it collapses superseded quotes to the freshest, paces the stream with a token bucket, and when a consumer is full it skips rather than blocks — every skipped update counted, never silently lost. The pricer is never back-pressured by a slow downstream.
