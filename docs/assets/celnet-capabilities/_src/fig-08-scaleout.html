An intra-fleet highest-random-weight (rendezvous) partition map shards the trading universe deterministically across nodes. Adding or removing a node remaps only its share of the portfolio — the rest stays put.

Raft-replicated state & hot-standby takeover
Each node's book state is replicated through a full Raft log — leader election, log-matching with conflicting-tail truncation, compaction and the InstallSnapshot RPC — committing only on quorum and replaying bit-identically (to_bits) on any follower, so a hot standby takes over with zero committed loss.
election · truncation compaction · InstallSnapshot bit-identical replay
Cross-fleet risk fan-out
Firm risk aggregates across shards over the HRW map — additive Greeks and the vega ladder merge associatively, while non-additive VaR / ES / FRTB curvature re-gather their constituents and re-derive once. The fan-out result equals the single-node aggregate to 1e-12.
