Layer 2
Market
Carry — a forward / discount producer, not a branch
Carry answers only how the underlying drifts and discounts, producing forward(t) and discount(t). Two-rate FX, equity dividend-and-repo, commodity convenience and crypto funding all collapse to generalized cost-of-carry F = S · eb·t, differing only in how b is assembled from named factors. The established FX carry reproduces its forward and discount bit-for-bit.

Layer 3
Payoff
Asset-class-agnostic payoff engines
Vanilla, barrier, Asian, structured and multi-asset engines consume only forward(t), discount(t) and a surface — never the carry variant. A payoff engine that branches on the carry kind is a review blocker, not a shortcut. A new asset class is a new carry builder with zero payoff-engine edits.
Risk-factor sensitivities stay a fixed zero-alloc shape in the pinned hot core; the cross-asset factor decomposition (GIRR · equity · commodity · CSR) lives off the hot path in the factor-generic risk layer — "general" reconciled with "zero-alloc".
