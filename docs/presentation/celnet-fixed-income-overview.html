Why it hangs together

One contract, one core, many surfaces

Fixed income is not a bolt-on module. It is the same pricing dispatch, the same risk cube, the same fan-out and the same wire contract that FX options use, entered through a different lens. That is the governing tenet: never fork a per-asset-class path — generalise the seam.

01
One wire contract

A single, current, unversioned schema. The GUI, the FIX counterparty, the Rust SDK, Excel and the CLI all speak it — so a capability lands on all five surfaces or it is not done.

02
One hot core

A pinned, lock-free, zero-allocation pricing path fed by a busy-polling ingest ring, physically separated from the async edge. Bonds, swaps and options share it.

03
One risk spine

Fills route into a firm-wide hierarchy of risk portfolios; risk is normalised to a convention-free canonical form, rolled up in one cube, and exited by a trader-authored policy.

04
Configured, not coded

Pricing pipelines, tiering, routing rules, acceptance rules and hedge policies are all drag-and-drop decision graphs a trader composes — no release needed to change desk behaviour.

05
Everything is observable

Per-stage p50/p99 of the tick → quote pipeline, per-LP win rates, and a per-lift trace stitching price → order → risk → hedge under one id.

06
Built for the whole book

Sized for investment-banking portfolios and high-performance counterparties from day one: horizontal scale-out, batch pricing across many instruments and tenors.

Rules of the road

Domain tabselects the lens, not a separate app. A cross-asset screen appears under both trading tabs and renders its FI form here.
Rail sectiongroups rows by the question they answer: where is the market, how do we price it, what do we hold, how does risk leave.

What this screen is

What this screen is
1

Risk is bucketed and measured against a warehouse threshold. Below it the desk keeps the risk on purpose; above it, the overflow has to go.

2

Latency discipline
Tail latency is the product

A pinned, busy-polling hot core with no allocation, no locks and no logging on the path, joined to the async edge only by wait-free rings. Telemetry offloads over a bounded queue so measuring does not cost you the thing you are measuring.

Correctness
Conventions before cleverness

FI pricing errors are convention errors — day count, settlement, schedule roll. Those are first-class configuration, and numerical results are validated against an independent reference, never merely asserted plausible.

Continuity
Upgrades without dropping a client

Blue-green handoff with live-state transfer: no dropped connections, no in-flight order loss. The book is journalled with deterministic crash recovery and can be replicated with quorum commit and hot-standby failover.

Scale-out
Sharded, not scaled-up

Risk fans out across shards over a partition map, and the fan-out result is designed to equal the single-node answer exactly.

Control
Deny by default

Capabilities are granted per action × asset class; information barriers prune data before it is ever aggregated, and admin surfaces are delegable without handing over the keys.

Openness
Your models, our engine

Desks run their own pricing models inside the engine through a sandboxed, fuel-metered plugin surface — deterministic, replayable, and bounded in runtime.

In one picture

The whole thing, said briefly

Prices arrive from many providers and become one book. That book, plus a curve the desk owns, becomes a client's price through a pipeline the desk composed. When a client lifts it, the fill is accepted by rule, booked, and routed into a risk portfolio. What the desk does not want to keep leaves through a policy it drew itself — as a real trade. Every step of that is measured, attributable, and reachable from all five clients.
